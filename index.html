Helpful Horse 🐴

This page is just a normal webpage with ordinary content. The horse you (should) see walking around is injected by a single script tag - everything it does lives inside #helpfulhorse-root, it never touches the rest of the page, and clicks pass straight through it except when they land on the horse itself.

Right-click the horse for a menu, or just drag it around.

To add it to your own site:

<script src="helpfulhorse.js" defer></script>

See README.md for hosting and configuration options.
